About MediPredict Pro

Clinical Machine Learning decision support platform designed for preliminary risk assessment.

🏥 Medical Disclaimer

MediPredict Pro is an educational and clinical demonstration tool designed to assist healthcare professionals in preliminary risk stratification. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, clinical judgment, or laboratory diagnostic testing. Always consult a qualified medical professional for health evaluations.

📚 Model & Dataset Specifications

  • Diabetes Model: Trained on the Pima Indians Diabetes Dataset using Support Vector Machines (SVM).
  • Heart Disease Model: Trained on the Cleveland Heart Disease Dataset using Logistic Regression.
  • Parkinson's Model: Trained on the Oxford Vocal Acoustics Dataset using Support Vector Classification (SVC).
